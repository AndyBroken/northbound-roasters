Our Story

Started in a garage.
Still obsessed.

In 2014, two friends roasted their first batch on a modified popcorn popper. Today Northbound supplies 180+ cafés and offices — but we still taste every single batch.

What We Believe

Three things we won’t compromise on

Transparency

We publish what we pay every farm, every year. No vague “ethically sourced” claims — just numbers.

Freshness

Roasted to order, shipped in 48 hours. Coffee is produce — it should be treated that way.

People First

Living wages for our team, long-term contracts for farmers, and real support for our café partners.

Milestones

A decade of good coffee

2014

First roast in a SE Portland garage. Sold out at the Saturday market.

2017

Opened our Division Street café & bought a real roaster.

2021

Launched our direct-trade program with 6 partner farms.

2025

Named Roaster of the Year. 180+ wholesale partners nationwide.
