FAQ

Common questions

How fresh is your coffee when it ships?

We roast Monday through Friday and ship within 48 hours of roasting. Most orders arrive within 2–4 days anywhere in the continental U.S.

Do you offer whole bean and ground?

Yes — every coffee is available whole bean or ground for drip, espresso, pour-over or French press. Just mention it with your order.

What are your wholesale minimums?

Our wholesale program starts at 10 lbs per week. Partners get tiered pricing, free equipment consultation and on-site barista training in the Portland area.

Is your packaging recyclable?

Our bags are certified home-compostable, including the valve. Shipping boxes are 100% recycled cardboard.

Can I visit the roastery?

Absolutely. We host free public cuppings every Saturday at 10am at our Division Street café — no reservation needed.
